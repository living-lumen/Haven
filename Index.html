About Living Lumen

Living Lumen is a growing creative project by David, a young interior design enthusiast. The idea is simple: a home should feel alive — filled with light, warmth, and individuality. This portfolio is a space to showcase design ideas and practice creating timeless interiors.

Living Lumen is a student-led creative project, not yet a registered business.
